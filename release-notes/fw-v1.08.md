Firmware **v1.08** for the LilyGO **T-Beam Supreme** with the u-blox **MAX-M10S** GPS (not the L76K version) checks the microSD card's FAT32 structures before writing to it. If the check fails, the tracker reports E4011 (`SD card damaged`), leaves the card untouched and records trips and diagnostics on internal flash when available, so you can copy your files off the card on a PC before reformatting it. Install server v1.21 first so it accepts the new code. As a precaution while the cause of a FAT wipe seen on one card is still unconfirmed, a mounted card now stays powered for half a second after it is unmounted at power-off, and after a reset the tracker waits half a second before cutting card power if the card may still be finishing a write. These changes have host tests only; bench and road checks remain to be done. The tracker reports E4006 when a GPX write, sync or close fails on microSD or internal flash.
