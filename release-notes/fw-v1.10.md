Firmware **v1.10** for the LilyGO **T-Beam Supreme** with the u-blox **MAX-M10S** GPS checks that the GPS power rail, battery detection and power-chip measurements are enabled at startup and reports E2010 if it cannot verify them after three attempts. The power-chip library's existing initialization sets TS to fixed input a few I2C transactions before the charger pause. The firmware checks and repairs that setting only after confirming the pause, so TS stays out of charger decisions. The tracker checks that the microSD power rail is off at boot and enabled at 3.3 V before mounting. It also checks that the backup battery charger is off. It gives a mounted microSD card 500 ms to settle after unmounting it on a failed storage check and tries to power it off between degraded storage retries, with a serial warning if three attempts cannot confirm power-off. Internal flash can take over recording only when its `/trips` folder opens as a directory. Deploy server **v1.25** so Device health shows the warning and its description when E2010 occurs, and the monitor can send alerts for it.
