Firmware **v1.10** for the LilyGO **T-Beam Supreme** with the u-blox **MAX-M10S** GPS checks that the GPS power rail and power-chip measurements are enabled at startup and reports E2010 if it cannot verify them after three attempts. The tracker checks that the microSD power rail is enabled at 3.3 V before mounting. It gives a mounted microSD card 500 ms to settle after unmounting it on a failed storage check and tries to power it off between degraded storage retries, with a serial warning if three attempts cannot confirm power-off. Internal flash can take over recording only when its `/trips` folder opens as a directory. Deploy server **v1.25** before installing this firmware so the server can accept E2010.
