Firmware **v1.10** for the LilyGO **T-Beam Supreme** with the u-blox **MAX-M10S** GPS switches new recordings to internal flash after repeated microSD write failures and keeps uploading readable trips from the card. It recovers complete points from failed trip starts, repairs junk after a GPX closing tag before upload, and gives later trips a turn when earlier uploads keep failing. When powering off, the tracker can interrupt a slow upload reply and waits for the file to close before unmounting. GPS timestamps reject impossible dates and work past January 2038. These changes have host tests only. Bench and road checks remain.
