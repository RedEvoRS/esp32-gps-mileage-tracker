Firmware **v1.09** for the LilyGO **T-Beam Supreme** with the u-blox **MAX-M10S** GPS (not the L76K version) fixes sixteen bugs found in a code audit, in the diagnostics journal, trip recording and upload, and power and charging. Journal: startup lists `/diag` in a single pass and keeps its place when GPS writes interrupt it, so weeks of stored diagnostic segments no longer stall microSD loading; cleanup also evicts the oldest sealed segment at the 512-segment limit; dropped-event counts no longer underflow after a cancelled read-back; one-time snapshot enrollment consumes its ID only after a journal save succeeds; and power-off seals open segments promptly on a full RAM queue, giving up on failed journal writes after three attempts in a row that make no progress, inside the existing 15-second shutdown window. Trips: after repeated microSD write failures, new recordings move to internal flash while readable trips on the card keep uploading; complete points are recovered from failed trip starts, junk after a GPX closing tag is repaired before upload, later trips get a turn when earlier uploads keep failing, power-off can interrupt a slow upload reply and waits for the file to close before unmounting, and GPS timestamps reject impossible dates and work past January 2038. Power: power-chip interrupts are cleared with checked writes and retried without repeating their actions, and USB power is rechecked every 15 s in case an interrupt is missed. At startup the charger switch-off is confirmed by read-back; if it can't be confirmed within 5 s, startup carries on and reports E2008 (`Charge control fail`), because charging may still be on while storage starts. Charging stays off until its 4.1 V target reads back, with E2008 if it won't confirm, and unreadable or implausible charge-temperature readings pause charging without false E2004/E2005 alerts. Startup uses one microSD capacity check and keeps a card that fails it out of use, recording on internal flash instead, and the tracker reports E4003 (`No recording storage available`) when neither store works, at startup or while running. No server update is needed beyond v1.21, which v1.08 already required. These changes have host tests only; bench and road checks remain to be done.
